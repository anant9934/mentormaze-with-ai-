# MentorMaze --- Evidence-Based Market Pricing & Competitive Benchmark

**Research date:** 12 November 2026\
**Market:** India\
**Target segment:** University students, fresh graduates and
early-career professionals\
**Phase 1:** Human mentorship and human-led mock interviews\
**Phase 2:** AI mentorship and AI mock interviews

> Research method: Public competitor pricing pages and Topmate's
> published paid-booking aggregates were reviewed. Advertised prices are
> snapshots and can change. Public prices do not prove booking volume or
> quality unless the provider publishes that data. AI practice and
> human-delivered sessions are treated as different products.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 1. Executive Decision

MentorMaze should position itself **below established paid human
mentorship benchmarks, but not at unsustainably low prices**.

## Recommended public launch prices

  Product                                              Duration   Recommended price
  ----------------------------------- ------------------------- -------------------
  Mentor discovery                                          ---                Free
  Quick clarity call                                     15 min            **₹149**
  Standard mentorship                                    30 min            **₹249**
  Deep mentorship                                        60 min            **₹449**
  Human mock interview --- Starter                   30--40 min            **₹299**
  Human mock interview --- Standard                  45--60 min            **₹399**
  Human mock interview --- Detailed     60 min + written report            **₹499**

These are proposed launch prices---not proven optimal prices. They
deliberately sit below Career Call's listed 15/30/60-minute prices and
below MockitX's ₹499 human mock slot, while staying within a credible
human-service price range.

**Do not make ₹99 the default 30-minute mentorship price.** It undercuts
the market substantially and leaves little room for a mentor payout,
gateway costs, refunds and platform support.

# 2. Competitor Pricing --- Current Public Snapshots

## 2.1 Career Call --- direct mentorship competitor

Career Call's public pricing page states "No subscription. Pay per call"
and publishes these starting prices:

  Product           Duration   Public starting price
  --------------- ---------- -----------------------
  Quick clarity       15 min                    ₹199
  Deep dive           30 min                    ₹349
  Full session        60 min                    ₹599

The listed inclusions are important: - 15-minute call: one focused
question and a WhatsApp recap. - 30-minute call: written summary, next
steps and seven-day text follow-up. - 60-minute call: detailed plan,
14-day text follow-up and one free 15-minute check-in.

Source: https://careercall.in/pricing

**What this means for MentorMaze:** A ₹149 / ₹249 / ₹449 price ladder is
meaningfully cheaper while retaining a recognizable service structure.
Do not copy their promise of a refund if the service definition or
operational ability differs.

## 2.2 Topmate --- paid marketplace benchmark

Topmate's own market report uses paid booking records, not merely listed
prices. Its published window is 1 November 2025 to 31 October 2026,
updated 1 November 2026.

  Category                            Paid 1:1 calls   Median paid price     Middle 50%
  --------------------------------- ---------------- ------------------- --------------
  Software engineering mentorship             14,375                ₹498     ₹204--₹910
  Career coaching                             11,695                ₹609   ₹310--₹1,200
  Mock interviews                              5,037                ₹809   ₹459--₹1,500
  Data science mentorship                      4,308                ₹510   ₹309--₹1,000
  Product management mentorship                4,219                ₹709   ₹409--₹1,400

For software engineering mentorship: - 30 minutes is the most common 1:1
call length (43%). - 55% of paid bookings in the category were 1:1
calls. - The marketplace also sells packages and priority DMs.

For mock interviews: - 60 minutes is the most common 1:1 call length
(61%). - The category median is ₹809.

Sources: - https://topmate.io/reports -
https://topmate.io/reports/cost/software-engineering-mentorship -
https://topmate.io/reports/cost/mock-interview

**Interpretation:** ₹249 for a standard 30-minute session is a
deliberate student-accessible discount versus the ₹498 software
engineering median. ₹399--₹499 for a human mock interview is below the
₹809 median, but should include a clear scope and a competent
interviewer.

Caveat: Topmate's report reflects its current listed expert population
and paid bookings, not every Indian mentorship transaction. It is a
strong platform-specific benchmark, not a complete census of India.

## 2.3 Incruit --- low-price human mock interview

Incruit's public page advertises: - ₹149 per interview. - 90-minute 1:1
mock interview. - Experienced interview coach. - Structured post-session
feedback. - Human-led interview.

Source: https://www.incruit.online/

**Interpretation:** This is an aggressive price anchor. It is not safe
to assume the service duration, mentor seniority, interviewer
availability, feedback depth and operational model are identical to
MentorMaze. Validate the actual booking flow and terms before treating
it as a directly comparable unit price.

MentorMaze should not blindly price at ₹149 for a 60-minute human
interview. That would create a severe supply-economics challenge unless
mentors volunteer, are subsidized or the format is group-based.

## 2.4 MockitX --- human mock interview

MockitX publicly advertises: - ₹499 per slot. - Real human HR and
technical mentors. - Resume, role and job-description customization. -
Written score and actionable feedback. - No mandatory subscription.

Sources: - https://mockitx.com/ - https://mockitx.com/pricing/

**Interpretation:** ₹499 is a useful direct human mock-interview
benchmark. MentorMaze can enter at ₹399 for a standard session and ₹499
for a more detailed report-based session.

## 2.6 Free alternative --- ADPList

ADPList offers a free mentorship model, creating a meaningful substitute
for students.

Source: https://adplist.org/

**Interpretation:** Students can already seek free advice. MentorMaze
must make the paid value obvious: - relevant mentor matching, - verified
experience, - reliable scheduling, - prepared sessions, - structured
action plans, - continuity and progress tracking.

Do not make every basic profile view or mentor discovery action paid.

# 6. Mentor Supply and Marketplace Economics

Low student prices are only useful if qualified mentors accept the
payout.

## 6.1 Suggested commission experiment

Test a 10--15% platform commission on completed bookings. Do not
finalize the commission until Cashfree gateway fees, Easy Split fees,
GST treatment, refunds and settlement mechanics are confirmed.

For early founding mentors: - No application fee. - No mandatory
subscription. - Temporary lower commission may be offered with a defined
end date or booking threshold. - Show the exact expected payout before
mentor publishes a price. - Never reduce mentor payout after a booking
without the agreed policy.

## 6.2 Why the ₹149 price point is risky

At 10% commission, the platform earns only ₹14.90 before payment fees,
taxes, support, refunds and infrastructure.

At ₹249, the platform earns ₹24.90 before costs.

At ₹399, the platform earns ₹39.90 before costs.

A fixed payment processing fee can materially reduce margin on small
tickets. Consider: - payment fee allocation under the gateway
contract, - bundles to reduce payment events per delivered session, -
platform-funded promotions only for a limited acquisition budget, -
minimum payout floors negotiated with mentors.

Do not assume a 10% commission yields a 10% net margin.

## 6.3 Illustrative gross commission

Assuming a simplified 12% commission on customer price, before tax and
other costs:

    Customer price   Gross platform commission
  ---------------- ---------------------------
              ₹149                      ₹17.88
              ₹249                      ₹29.88
              ₹299                      ₹35.88
              ₹399                      ₹47.88
              ₹449                      ₹53.88
              ₹499                      ₹59.88

These are arithmetic illustrations, not tax or settlement calculations.
Build a proper payment ledger and confirm marketplace settlement
obligations with Cashfree and a qualified accountant.

# 8. Launch Price Experiments

Do not assume the recommended price is optimal. Test it with actual paid
bookings.

## Test A --- 30-minute mentorship

-   Variant 1: ₹199
-   Variant 2: ₹249
-   Keep duration, mentor profile quality and deliverables comparable.

## Test B --- human mock interview

-   Variant 1: ₹349
-   Variant 2: ₹399
-   Keep interviewer experience, duration and report format comparable.

## Test C --- bundles

-   Single session versus three-session bundle.
-   Measure whether bundle buyers complete all sessions and rebook.

Track: 1. Mentor profile views. 2. Booking conversion. 3. Checkout
completion. 4. Mentor acceptance. 5. Completed-session rate. 6.
Cancellation and no-show. 7. Refund rate. 8. 30/60-day repeat booking.
9. Student satisfaction. 10. Mentor earnings per active hour. 11.
Contribution margin per completed session.

Do not optimize only for clicks or signups. The best price is the one
that produces completed, satisfactory sessions while keeping mentors
willing to remain active.

# 9. Final Decision

Use this as MentorMaze's initial public pricing:

  Product                                    Price
  ----------------------------------- ------------
  Mentor discovery                            Free
  Quick Clarity --- 15 min                **₹149**
  Standard Mentorship --- 30 min          **₹249**
  Deep Mentorship --- 60 min              **₹449**
  Human Mock Interview --- Starter        **₹299**
  Human Mock Interview --- Standard       **₹399**
  Human Mock Interview --- Detailed       **₹499**
  3 mentorship sessions                   **₹699**
  5 mentorship sessions                 **₹1,149**
  3 starter mock interviews               **₹849**
  3 standard mock interviews            **₹1,099**

## Why this is the recommended balance

-   Lower than Career Call's published 15/30/60-minute prices.
-   Lower than the Topmate software engineering mentorship paid-call
    median.
-   Below MockitX's ₹499 human mock-interview price for the standard
    offer.
-   Below Topmate's ₹809 mock-interview median.
-   Not pretending human sessions can match AI mock interview unit
    economics.
-   Leaves room for experienced mentors to charge more.

**Launch these prices as a controlled pilot, not as permanent market
truth.** Recruit the first 10--15 mentors, agree on minimum acceptable
payouts, then validate the first 50--100 completed paid bookings. Revise
prices only after observing conversion, mentor acceptance and
contribution margin.
