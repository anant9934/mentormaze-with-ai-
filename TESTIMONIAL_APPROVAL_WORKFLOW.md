## 1. Compliance Policy & Truth-in-Advertising Standard

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Mandatory Verification & Anti-Fabrication Rule**  
> Under MentorMaze platform governance and advertising standards:
> 1. Statements drafted below are proposed perspectives created for the individual's review and approval.
> 2. **They must NEVER be published as genuine endorsements or confirmed quotations until each individual formally reviews and approves the wording.**
> 3. No star ratings or numerical review scores may be published unless explicitly submitted by the user through a verified session feedback flow. If no actual rating has been submitted, the rating element remains strictly hidden.

## 4. Consent & Approval Collection Workflow

Before flipping any draft statement to `Approved & Verified`:

1. **Direct Communication Outreach:**  
   Send the individual their specific draft along with:
   - Full proposed quote
   - Name and exact designation/affiliation
   - Portrait photo to be displayed
2. **Review & Revision Window:**  
   Allow the individual to edit, refine, or rewrite the statement in their own voice.
3. **Written Consent Form / Email Confirmation:**  
   Obtain documented affirmative consent (email reply, signed media release, or in-app verification).
4. **Production Promotion:**  
   Only after written sign-off is logged in this registry should the status be updated to `Approved & Verified`.
